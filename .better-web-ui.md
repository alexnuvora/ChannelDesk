# ChannelDesk design context

## Audience
Social media managers, creators, small-business teams and agencies managing multiple social channels from one workspace. They work in the product frequently and need to scan publishing state, conversations, performance and approvals quickly.

## Core jobs
- Create, review, schedule and publish social content.
- Monitor a publishing calendar and resolve failures.
- Reply to conversations without switching apps.
- Understand channel/content performance.
- Manage media, reports, competitors, Smart Links, automations and connections.
- Use ChatGPT/MCP as a first-class control surface without losing dashboard clarity or permissions.

## Brand personality
Editorial, confident, intelligent and calm. Premium productivity software rather than a generic AI dashboard. Precise and grown-up, with enough warmth to feel creative. Avoid neon AI styling, purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, giant rounded cards, excessive pills and repetitive card grids.

## Visual direction
Editorial control room: warm paper-like neutrals, ink typography, a restrained acid-chartreuse signature accent inherited from ChannelDesk, compact information-dense work surfaces, strong typographic hierarchy and selective borders. Use whitespace and rules before card containers. Functional surfaces remain clarity-first.

## Product principles
Hierarchy before decoration. One obvious primary action per surface. Common actions stay close to their content. Status uses text/icon/shape as well as color. Critical functionality remains reachable on mobile. Empty states teach the next action. Never imply integrations or features that are not actually implemented.
